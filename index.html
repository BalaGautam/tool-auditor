How to build the board
  1. Base: lay a flat piece of cardboard on the floor. Glue the white chart paper to it with a glue stick or double-sided tape across the whole sheet so it lies flat. Nails tear the cardboard and the paper wrinkles.
  2. Board edge: keep the whole white sheet in every photo, with the cardboard (or any darker background) showing around it. The app finds the paper's four corners and straightens the photo, so you don't need to stand in the same spot each time. On a light background, draw a solid black square (about 3 cm) at each corner instead, 2–3 cm in from the edges with white paper around it; the app looks for these first.
  3. Outlines: lay the tools out with a finger's width between them. Trace each one with a thick marker, then fill the shape with a bright color (red or orange). When a tool is gone, its colored shape is easy to see.
  4. Slot codes: mark the slots in step 2 and press Auto-number. Codes run A1, A2… across the top row, then B1… (or from the bottom row up, if you tick that option). Write each code next to its outline.
  5. Photos: stand over the board and hold the phone flat, with all four markers in frame. Use even daylight, no flash, and keep your shadow off the board.

2Planogram: mark the slots

Drag a box around each tool on the straightened photo and give it a name. Part number and unit cost are optional; they appear on restock lines. The planogram saves in this browser, and you can export it to share or back it up.
